      Why not just let the SDK choose?

      Firebase AI Logic has a mode, PREFER_ON_DEVICE, that falls back to the cloud on its own. If the on-device model were missing or still downloading, that mode would have sent your whole letter to the cloud. Plain Letter never does that: it uses ONLY_ON_DEVICE for your letter, and the cloud model only ever gets the masked copy you approved.

      Answers from the on-device model are checked before you see them. If it answers in the wrong language or leaves out part of the answer, that answer is not shown, and Plain Letter offers the masked cloud path instead.
